# Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze retail sales data across customers, products, locations, shipping methods, and time periods.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into structured visual insights, making it easier to identify patterns in sales performance, customer activity, regional distribution, and product performance.

---

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall retail sales performance.
- Compare sales across different regions.
- Examine sales distribution by shipping method.
- Evaluate yearly sales trends.
- Identify high-performing cities.
- Analyze product sub-category performance.
- Track customer and order activity.
- Present business data through clear and interactive visualizations.

---

## Dashboard Analysis

### Sales by Region

The dashboard compares sales performance across different geographic regions, allowing regional performance to be evaluated and compared.

### Geographic Distribution

A map visualization is used to display retail activity across different states, providing a geographic view of the dataset.

### Sales by Ship Mode

The dashboard analyzes how sales are distributed across the available shipping methods.

### Customer and Order Metrics

Key performance indicators are used to summarize customer and order activity, providing a quick overview of the scale of the retail operations represented in the dataset.

### Sales Trend Analysis

Sales are analyzed across different years to identify changes and trends in overall performance over time.

### Sales by City

City-level analysis is used to compare sales contribution across different locations and identify areas with higher sales activity.

### Product Sub-Category Analysis

Product sub-categories are compared to understand how different product groups contribute to overall sales performance.
